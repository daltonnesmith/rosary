    Praying the rosary.

    Begin with the Sign of the Cross, the Apostles' Creed, one Our Father, three Hail Marys for faith, hope, and charity, and the Glory Be. Each of the five mysteries has one Our Father, ten Hail Marys, and a Glory Be, followed by the optional Fatima prayer. Close with the Hail Holy Queen, the concluding prayer, and the Sign of the Cross.

    Joyful mysteries are suggested on Monday and Saturday; Sorrowful on Tuesday and Friday; Glorious on Wednesday and Sunday; Luminous on Thursday. Seasonal and personal customs may differ. You can choose any set.

    The English prayers here use traditional public-domain wording, including the traditional Apostles' Creed, not a modern copyrighted liturgical translation. The four mystery sets follow the customary Catholic rosary. The optional Fatima prayer is a traditional devotional prayer.

    Private, by design.

    Your history, place, and notes stay in this browser's local storage. Nothing is sent to a server. They do not automatically sync between devices or between a browser and an installed app. Private browsing, uninstalling, clearing site data, or switching the site's address may remove or separate them. Export a backup before making changes.

    Backups contain your notes in plain text: keep them somewhere private. Imports are validated and merged without deleting your existing entries. If an imported note differs from an existing note with the same ID, both are kept.
