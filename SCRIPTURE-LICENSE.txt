Scripture source and notice

The application bundles selected, verbatim verse text from the World English
Bible, Catholic Edition, published by eBible.org and dedicated to the public
domain. "World English Bible" is a trademark of eBible.org; Stillwater is an
independent companion, not an official publication or endorsement.

Source: https://ebible.org/Scriptures/eng-web-c_vpl.zip
Translation: https://ebible.org/eng-web-c/
Public-domain notice: https://ebible.org/eng-web-c/copyright.htm

The publisher permits copying, publishing, distributing, sharing and using this
translation. Its notice asks that changed Bible text not be called the World
English Bible. The bundled Scripture wording is not rewritten or paraphrased.
Formatting, chapter selection and division into five readings are Stillwater's.
Source footnotes and empty verse entries are omitted; nonempty verse text and
its numbering are retained. The source-text SHA-256 is in src/scripture.js.

The plan contains the seven Catholic deuterocanonical books, including Baruch 6
(the Letter of Jeremiah). Esther's Greek additions are presented in the context
of complete Greek-edition chapters 1, 3, 4, 5, 8 and 10; Daniel's additions are
presented in complete chapters 3, 13 and 14. These collections do not claim to
contain every chapter of Esther or Daniel. All four Gospels are included.

To reproduce the dataset, download and extract eng-web-c_vpl.txt from the source
archive, then run node scripts\prepare-scripture.mjs <path-to-that-file>.
The build never downloads Scripture; the generated dataset is checked in.
