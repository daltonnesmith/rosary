Stillwater liturgical calendar source notice

The bundled src/liturgical-data.js is generated from romcal and its
UnitedStates_En calendar, both pinned to 3.0.0-dev.144 (MIT license).
Source: https://github.com/romcal/romcal
The normalization script retains daily celebrations in source precedence order,
including optional observances, seasons, weeks, ranks and vestment colors.
Both Sunday and Thursday Ascension variants are calculated by romcal.
The generator adds the permitted optional Blessed Virgin Mary memorial on
unimpeded Saturdays of Ordinary Time (General Norms for the Liturgical Year
and the Calendar, no. 15), and includes the U.S. white vestment option for
All Souls, as shown in the USCCB calendar. Privileged-weekday commemorations
retain their weekday color in the display; an empty source color for such
a commemoration must not be interpreted as "no Mass."
Only the normalized static data is shipped to the browser. No calendar API is
contacted, and the generator is not needed to build or run the checked-in app.

Coverage: civil years 2024-2035, calculated using the bundled current rules.
This is not an archival reconstruction of decrees in force in each past year,
an official diocesan ordo, or a determination of holy days of obligation.
Future decrees can change future calendars. Parish, diocesan, religious-order,
dedication and patronal observances can differ. This is a daytime guide, not a
complete listing of Masses or evening vigils. Holy Thursday and the Easter
Vigil have explanatory notes in the app.

Selected movable dates, national observances and recent additions were
cross-checked against the USCCB's published 2026, 2027 and 2028 calendars:
https://www.usccb.org/committees/divine-worship/liturgical-calendar
https://www.usccb.org/resources/2026cal.pdf
https://www.usccb.org/resources/2027cal.pdf
https://www.usccb.org/resources/2028cal.pdf
Those publications are references, not redistributed application content.

To reproduce the checked-in dataset with Node.js 22+:
  npm ci
  npm run prepare:calendar
  npm test
Review updated official calendars before updating the pinned generator or
extending coverage. Update this notice and the app cache for a data release.
